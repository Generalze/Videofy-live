# French translation review — 40 items

Thank you. You are the only instrument that can settle this.

Automatic checks have already been run and they are **deliberately not shown to
you**. On the Nigerian languages our own checker was wrong three times in ways
that each changed the ranking, and knowing what a machine concluded biases what
a person sees. Please judge only what is in front of you.

## What this is for

C7 (Videofy Live) translates private messages and live calls. Your answers
decide whether we are allowed to switch French on for real users. If the answer
is no, we use nothing — an untranslated call is recoverable, a confidently
wrong one is not.

Translation runs **during a live call**, so a sentence you mark wrong is one
somebody may act on within seconds, without ever seeing the original.

## How to review

Open `review-fr.csv` in Excel, Google Sheets or LibreOffice. Each row has:

- **source_text** — what was written
- **translation_to_review** — what the machine produced
- **direction** — `en->fr` means English was translated INTO French;
  `fr->en` means French was translated into English

Fill in columns 1 to 10. Please answer every yes/no question, even where the
answer feels obvious.

## What we most need you to catch

Mark these hard, because they are the ones that cost somebody money or a
meeting:

- **Dates, days and times.** A day, an hour or a relative word like *demain*
  that is missing, moved or turned into something else.
- **Numbers and identifiers.** Amounts, account numbers, phone numbers, codes.
  A digit added, dropped or reordered is a failure even if the sentence reads
  perfectly.
- **Negation.** A sentence that says the opposite of the source.
- **Invented content.** Anything present in the translation that was not in the
  source, however plausible it sounds.

Two rows are not sentences at all — an emoji and a bare number. Judge what the
machine did with them as you would anything else; they are there on purpose.

## The question that decides it

Column 8, *"Would you trust this in a real private chat?"*, carries the most
weight. A translation can be grammatical, natural and still be one you would
not stake a real arrangement on. Answer that one as yourself, not as a marker.

## What happens next

Your scores are recorded against the route with your name, the date and the
method, and they are what permits or refuses French translation in production.
If you are not confident about an item, say so in column 10 rather than
guessing — an honest blank is more useful to us than a filled cell.
